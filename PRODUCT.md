# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Customers in and around Kukusan, Beji, Depok (Greater Jakarta) who find Toko Matcha through Instagram Reels and TikTok, then tap the link on a phone. Mostly students and young professionals from the nearby Universitas Indonesia campus. They arrive mid-scroll, on mid-range Android hardware, over patchy mobile data, and they are deciding one thing: is this worth the trip today.

A smaller second audience is desktop visitors evaluating the shop as a brand: potential collaborators, food press, and the owner sharing the page.

## Product Purpose

A single-page landing site for Toko Matcha, a real matcha shop at Jl. K.H.M Usman No.81 A, Kukusan, Kec. Beji, Kota Depok. It exists to convert an Instagram or TikTok tap into a visit or an order.

Success is a visitor who knows three things within one scroll: what the drink looks like, what it costs, and where the shop is.

## Brand Personality

Poured, unhurried, generous. The voice of someone who takes the drink seriously and takes themselves lightly. Not a tea ceremony, not a bubble-tea chain: a small shop that makes one thing properly and charges Rp15.000 for it.

Emotionally the page should feel cold, wet, and green. Condensation on a cup. Sky. The specific relief of a cold drink in Depok humidity.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: centered stack, three icon cards, gradient hero metric.
- Bubble-tea franchise sites: cluttered promo banners, stacked discount badges, rainbow palettes.
- Wellness-supplement aesthetics: sterile white, "clinically proven", stock leaves on gradient.
- Editorial-magazine drift: display-serif italic plus tracked-out mono labels plus ruled columns. The serif italic here is one lockup partner, not a magazine costume.
- Health-claim marketing. This is a drink shop, not a supplement brand. No "antioxidant rich", no "no crash", no "clinically".

## Design Principles

1. **Photograph the drink, don't describe it.** Imagery carries the page. Copy stays out of the way of the product.
2. **Claim only what is verifiable.** Address, price, and menu come from the real listing. Never invent grades, sourcing, or health benefits for a real business.
3. **Weight over speed.** The brand's motion is liquid settling, not UI snapping. Things arrive slowly and land once.
4. **One idea per fold.** Long scroll, deliberate pacing, a single dominant subject in each viewport.
5. **The phone is the real canvas.** Every effect must survive a mid-range Android at 60fps. Ambition is spent on choreography, not on filters.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA for text contrast. Dark green `#274a26` on cream `#fdf7dd` and cream on dark green both clear AA at body sizes.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables all entrance choreography, parallax, the ribbon marquee, and the badge rotation. The page must be fully legible and complete with zero motion.
- All interactive elements reachable and visible on keyboard focus, including the flavour picker, which is built from real `<button>` elements.
- Never rely on colour alone: the selected flavour carries an outline and an updated text label, not just a tint.
